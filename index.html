About the Project

This capstone project explores the application of motion capture technology in occupational therapy settings. By leveraging advanced data science techniques and motion tracking systems, we aim to provide objective measurements and insights that can enhance patient assessment and treatment outcomes.

Occupational therapy focuses on helping individuals develop, recover, or maintain daily living and work skills. Motion capture technology offers the potential to quantify patient movements, track progress over time, and provide data-driven recommendations for therapy interventions.

Project Objectives

Data Collection

Develop methods for capturing and recording patient movement data using motion capture technology.

Analysis Framework

Create data analysis pipelines to process motion capture data and extract meaningful metrics.

Visualization Tools

Build interactive visualizations to help therapists understand patient progress and movement patterns.

Clinical Integration

Develop workflows that integrate motion capture insights into existing occupational therapy practices.

Methodology

Our approach combines several key components:

  • Motion Capture Systems: Utilizing advanced sensors and cameras to track patient movements in 3D space.
  • Data Processing: Applying signal processing and machine learning techniques to clean and analyze movement data.
  • Statistical Analysis: Employing statistical methods to identify patterns, trends, and clinically significant changes.
  • User Interface Design: Creating intuitive dashboards for therapists to access and interpret patient data.
  • Validation Studies: Collaborating with occupational therapists to validate the clinical relevance of our findings.

Team

This project is being developed as part of the UCSD Data Science Capstone program. Our team consists of graduate students in Data Science, working in collaboration with occupational therapy professionals and motion capture technology experts.
